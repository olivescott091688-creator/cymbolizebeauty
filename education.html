About the Program

Training that changes
what you can offer.

There is a whole population of women walking out of salons empty-handed because their stylist doesn't know what to do with their hair. Women experiencing hair loss from GLP-1 medications, chemotherapy, alopecia, hormonal shifts, or simply aging. They have nowhere to go.

The Elevate Mesh and Meshless Integration method was created by Kelly Maxwell and Emmy Hassan. Cymonne traveled to London to train with Kelly and Emmy personally, and earned her official certification as an Elevate educator this year. She is now one of a select group of certified Elevate educators teaching this method across the country.

This specialty is one of the biggest gaps in the industry right now. The demand is enormous. The supply of qualified stylists is almost nonexistent. Cymonne teaches this method because she believes every woman experiencing hair loss deserves access to a stylist who actually knows how to help her.

E
Curriculum

What you'll
learn.

Six core modules: technique, consultation, assessment, psychology, business, and hands-on practice.

01
Mesh Integration
Technique
Master the primary technique for hair loss clients, from scalp prep to full installation. You'll learn mesh sizing, color matching, hair selection, and the step-by-step build process. Includes maintenance instructions to give clients and how to schedule follow-up care.
02
Meshless
Integration
The mesh-free approach to the same seamless result. Learn how to integrate hair directly with your client's natural hair without a mesh base, and how to determine which method is right for each individual client's pattern and goals.
03
Hair Loss
Consultations
How to assess clients, ask the right questions, and build trust with women who are in a vulnerable place. What to look for during a scalp evaluation, how to read hair loss patterns, and how to set honest expectations about outcomes and timelines.
04
Client Assessment
& Technique Selection
How to read the scalp, evaluate hair loss stage and type, and choose the right technique for each client's specific situation. Not every client is a mesh candidate. You'll learn how to recognize who is, who isn't, and what to recommend when the fit isn't right.
05
Psychology of
Hair Loss Clients
The emotional dimension of this work is arguably the most important module. Understanding the shame, grief, and isolation that hair loss clients often carry. How to create a safe, non-judgmental environment. How to communicate results honestly without crushing hope. How to support a client through the process.
06
Business &
Pricing Strategy
How to price specialty services correctly so you're compensated fairly for the skill and time involved. How to market to your ideal client without advertising. How to position yourself as a hair loss specialist, including your social presence, consultation language, and client intake process.
