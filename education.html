About the Program

Training That Changes What You Can Offer

There is a whole population of women walking out of salons empty-handed because their stylist doesn't know what to do with their hair. Women experiencing hair loss from GLP-1 medications, chemotherapy, alopecia, hormonal shifts, or simply aging — they have nowhere to go.

Cymonne built the Elevate Mesh and Meshless Integration program to change that. This is hands-on, practical training in the techniques and the mindset you need to serve these women — and build a thriving specialty practice in the process.

Curriculum

What You'll Learn

🔮
Mesh Integration Technique

Master the primary technique for hair loss clients — from prep to installation to maintenance instructions for your clients.

✨
Invisible Bead Wefts

The seamless, damage-free alternative for clients with fine or fragile hair.

🧵
Sew-In Methods

Traditional and modified sew-in techniques adapted for clients with thinning hair.

💬
Hair Loss Consultations

How to assess clients, ask the right questions, and build trust with women who are in a vulnerable place.

📋
Client Assessment

Reading the scalp, evaluating hair loss stage and type, and choosing the right technique for each client.

💰
Business & Pricing Strategy

How to price specialty services correctly, market to your ideal client, and position yourself as a hair loss specialist.
