The Origin Story

The moment that
changed everything.

"She wore a wig her whole life, not occasionally. Every day, without exception, in front of everyone she knew."

Cymonne's grandmother never let anyone see her without it. Not even family. No one ever saw her without it.

Then one day, a young Cymonne found her sitting alone, crying. Her grandmother was ashamed to remove the wig, even in private. She felt ugly. She felt like less of a woman, like the person she was supposed to be had been taken from her, and no one had given her a way to get it back.

Cymonne sat with her in that room and made a silent promise: one day, I will give women back what hair loss steals from them.

That promise became her calling. She chose a career in hair not for the styling or the artistry, though she excels at both, but because she understood in her bones what it meant for a woman to feel like herself again.

A Moment She'll Never Forget

The client who
called back in tears.

She'd been messaging on social media for months. Not booking, just talking. Sending messages. Asking questions. Working up to something.

When she finally came in, she didn't say much. Cymonne listened, assessed, and built her a complete hair system from scratch, right there, that day.

The phone rang a few hours later.

The woman was crying. She said she had been suicidal. She hadn't left her house in years. Her hair loss had taken not just her appearance, but her will to be present in her own life.

"You gave me a reason to love myself again."

Her words, on the phone that evening

That's why this work matters. That's what a hair appointment can be when the stylist truly understands what's at stake.

What We Stand For

Values that
guide every appointment.

Five principles that shape how Cymonne approaches every client, every consultation, and every result.

01
Compassion
Every woman who walks through the door deserves to be truly seen and heard. Hair loss is an intimate, often isolating experience, so Cymonne approaches every client with the patience and empathy she would want for someone she loves.
02
Discretion
Your hair journey is personal. It stays that way. Cymonne handles every consultation and every transformation with the privacy and confidentiality it deserves. Many clients share things in this chair they've never told anyone.
03
Education
Knowledge is power, for clients and for stylists. Cymonne explains every option clearly, trains the next generation of specialists, and believes that an informed client makes the best decisions for her own hair and health.
04
Luxury
An elevated, intentional experience from the moment you reach out. The environment, the communication, and the result all reflect the care this work deserves.
05
Natural Results
Hair that looks, moves, and feels like your own, because it should. Cymonne doesn't consider the job done until the result is undetectable in motion and in photographs. That's the standard.
