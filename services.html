02 Hair Replacement

Cymonne’s signature specialty.

Signature Specialty

Mesh Integration

Upon Consultation

A non-surgical hair restoration system for thinning or patchy hair. A breathable mesh base is custom-fitted to your scalp, your natural hair gently pulled through, then blended with human hair extensions matched to your color and texture. Built once; rebuilt only if you decide to switch up your look entirely.

Because every system is custom-built to your scalp, your density, and your goals, pricing is quoted personally at your consultation — never off a menu.

Begin With a Consultation

Meshless Integration

Upon Consultation

The same seamless integration concept, without the mesh base. Human hair is integrated directly with your own for full, natural-looking coverage — ideal for women who want a lighter, mesh-free foundation. Cymonne helps you decide between mesh and meshless at your consultation.

Begin With a Consultation

Mesh Integration Adjustment

Upon Consultation · Every 4–6 Weeks

Maintenance for existing systems. As your natural hair grows, the beads securing your system are tightened and repositioned to keep the mesh snug, comfortable, and seamless — protecting the health of your natural hair between full realignments.

Book This Service

Mesh Integration Realignment

Upon Consultation · Every 6 Months

Your system is fully removed and thoroughly cleansed, your natural hair is cleansed underneath, and the system is reinstalled — keeping both your system and your natural hair healthy and properly aligned without a full rebuild.

Book This Service

Custom Wig

Upon Consultation · Fully Bespoke

A fully bespoke wig built from the ground up. The base is hand-molded to the exact size and structure of your scalp, then hair is hand-sewn on row by row. You choose length, color, and style — and no two are ever the same.

03 Hair Extensions

Discreet volume, healthy hair.

Hidden Bead Extensions

Upon Consultation · No Glue · No Heat

A discreet extension technique: wefts sewn along rows attached to small silicone-lined beads, tucked beneath your own hair so nothing shows. Seamless, natural-looking volume and length — no glue, no heat, no damage. Length, volume, and color match all factor into your quote.

Begin With a Consultation

Hidden Bead Move-Up

Upon Consultation · Every 6–8 Weeks

Maintenance for existing rows. Each bead is loosened, moved up close to the scalp, and retightened — keeping your rows secure and natural as your hair grows. No rows removed.

Book This Service

K-Tips

Upon Consultation · Individual Strands

Individual strand extensions fused with a small keratin bond — each strand hand-made and customized to your density and texture. Maintenance typically up to every 12 weeks. K-Tips cannot be booked same-day: your hair is assessed, matched, and each strand made before your appointment.

Begin With a Consultation

Hair Extensions Removal

Upon Consultation · Any Method

Safe, professional removal using the proper solvents or tools for your method, followed by a thorough cleanse — so hair is detangled, healthy, and ready for reinstall or a fresh start.
