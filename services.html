What We Offer

Services Designed for Real Hair Loss

Not every stylist is equipped to serve women experiencing hair thinning. CymbolizeBeauty was built from the ground up for exactly that.

Most stylists install extensions the traditional way — and have no idea what to do when a client walks in with visible hair loss.

Cymonne trained specifically in techniques designed for fine, fragile, and thinning hair. Her specialty — Mesh Integration — is a method that works with your existing hair rather than requiring a thick base to attach to. It's the difference between a stylist who tries to adapt and one who was trained from day one to serve you.

Every service at CymbolizeBeauty starts with an assessment. Cymonne takes the time to understand your hair, your goals, and your comfort level before any work begins.

Whether you have alopecia, are experiencing thinning from GLP-1 medications, chemo treatment, hormonal changes, or simply have naturally fine hair — there is a solution here for you.

And if she's not the right fit for what you need, she'll tell you that honestly and point you in the right direction. That's the kind of stylist she is.

⭐ Signature Specialty

Mesh Integration

Mesh Integration is Cymonne's primary specialty — and the reason most of her clients find her. This technique uses a breathable mesh base that sits against the scalp and blends seamlessly with your existing hair, no matter how fine or sparse it may be. It's designed specifically for women experiencing hair loss, alopecia, or extreme thinning where traditional extensions simply aren't an option.

The result? Hair that moves naturally, looks completely real, and gives clients back the fullness and length they thought they'd lost for good.
