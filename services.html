The Philosophy

Cymonne trained specifically in techniques designed for fine, fragile, and thinning hair. Her specialty — Mesh Integration — is a method that works with your existing hair rather than requiring a thick base to attach to.

I
✦ Signature Specialty

Mesh
Integration.

A non-surgical hair restoration system for thinning or patchy hair. A breathable mesh base is custom-fitted to your scalp, with your natural hair gently pulled through it, then blended with human hair extensions matched to your color and texture. The result is fuller, seamless coverage that moves and feels like your own hair. Cymonne also offers Meshless Integration — the same seamless result, built without the mesh base, for women who prefer a lighter foundation.

Your system is built once and lasts through regular maintenance visits — it only gets rebuilt if you decide to switch up your look entirely. Adjustments every 4–6 weeks keep the mesh snug and seamless as your natural hair grows, and a realignment every 6 months fully removes, cleanses, and reinstalls the system to keep both it and your natural hair healthy.

Installation $900+ ($100 deposit)  ·  Adjustment $250+  ·  Realignment $450+
