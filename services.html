Services

Consultations

FaceTime Consultation

$50 · 30 minutes

A video call where Cymonne visibly assesses your problem areas and answers your questions about a recommended service — all without you having to leave home. The right first step if you’re not local, on the fence, or want to hear the plan before you commit to a studio visit.

In-Person Consultation

$75 · 60 minutes · Las Vegas studio

Meet at the studio for a visual and physical assessment. You’ll look at hair options together and build a plan of action — the recommended first step for anyone considering Mesh Integration or a custom system, since those services are impossible to accurately quote without touching your hair.

Hair Replacement

Mesh Integration

Price upon consultation

Cymonne’s signature specialty. A non-surgical hair restoration system for hair loss ranging from minimal to extreme: a breathable mesh base is custom-fitted to your scalp, your natural hair gently pulled through, then blended with human hair extensions matched to your color and texture. Built once; rebuilt only if you decide to switch up your look entirely.

Because every system is custom-built, pricing is quoted personally at your consultation — never off a menu.

Meshless Integration

Price upon consultation · see Cymonne

The same seamless integration concept, without the mesh base. Human hair is integrated directly with your own for full, natural-looking coverage — ideal for anyone who wants a lighter, mesh-free foundation. Cymonne helps you decide between mesh and meshless at your consultation.

Mesh Integration Adjustment

Price upon consultation · every 4–6 weeks

Maintenance for existing systems. As your natural hair grows, the beads securing your system are tightened and repositioned to keep the mesh snug, comfortable, and seamless — protecting the health of your natural hair between full realignments.

Mesh Integration Realignment

Price upon consultation · every 6 months

Your system is fully removed and thoroughly cleansed, your natural hair is cleansed underneath, and the system is reinstalled — keeping both your system and your natural hair healthy and properly aligned without a full rebuild.

Custom Wig

Price upon consultation · fully bespoke

A fully bespoke wig built from the ground up. The base is hand-molded to the exact size and structure of your scalp, then hair is hand-sewn on row by row. You choose length, color, and style — and no two are ever the same.

Hair Extensions

Hidden Bead Extensions

From $150

A discreet extension technique: wefts sewn along rows attached to small silicone-lined beads, tucked beneath your own hair so nothing shows. Seamless, natural-looking volume and length — no glue, no heat, no damage. Length, volume, and color match all factor into your quote.

Hidden Bead Move-Up

$150 · every 6–8 weeks

Maintenance for existing rows. Each bead is loosened, moved up close to the scalp, and retightened — keeping your rows secure and natural as your hair grows. No rows removed.

K-Tips

Price upon consultation · individual strands

Individual strand extensions fused with a small keratin bond — each strand hand-made and customized to your density and texture. Maintenance typically up to every 12 weeks. K-Tips cannot be booked same-day: your hair is assessed, matched, and each strand made before your appointment.

Hair Extensions Removal

From $100 · any method

Safe, professional removal using the proper solvents or tools for your method, followed by a thorough cleanse — so hair is detangled, healthy, and ready for reinstall or a fresh start.
