I
✦ Signature Specialty

Mesh
Integration.

A fine, breathable mesh base — custom-matched to your scalp tone — is placed gently against the scalp. Human hair is woven individually through the mesh openings, building coverage directly from the scalp outward.

Because the mesh itself becomes the structural anchor rather than your existing hair, it works on virtually any density — including areas of visible scalp that traditional extensions cannot reach. There is no traction on fragile existing strands, no heat or adhesive at the attachment point.

The open-weave construction keeps the scalp breathing naturally. The result moves, washes, and behaves exactly like natural hair. Clients consistently describe it as impossible to detect — in motion, in a ponytail, and in photographs.

Every service

Eight specialized services — all designed with hair health as the primary goal, not just the aesthetic outcome.

01
Mesh Integration
The primary hair loss solution. A breathable mesh base anchors directly to the scalp, accepting human hair woven through its openings — creating coverage on any density of hair, including areas of complete hair loss. No traction, no adhesive, no stress on fragile strands. Looks and behaves exactly like natural hair. Pricing at consultation — every scalp and system is unique.
Book
02
Invisible Bead Wefts
Flat silicone-lined beads secure weft panels against the scalp with no heat, no glue, and no chemicals at the attachment point. The beads sit flush — undetectable even in a high ponytail or updo. Ideal for fine-hair clients who need volume without any mechanical stress on existing strands. Each weft panel moves naturally and maintains the appearance of your own hair.
Book
03
K-Tips
Individual keratin-bonded strands placed one at a time, heat-fused close to the scalp for a flat, undetectable result. Each strand moves independently — the most natural look of any extension method. Best for clients with some existing hair density who want seamless density and length. The individual placement allows for custom density mapping across different areas of the head.
Book
04
Sew-In Extensions
Weft panels sewn onto braided tracks. For fine-hair and hair-loss clients, Cymonne uses a modified flat-track approach that distributes weight evenly across the scalp rather than concentrating tension at attachment points. No adhesive, no heat where it matters. Cymonne adapts this traditional method specifically to protect fragile strands — the technique is always adjusted based on your current hair density and condition.
Book
05
Brazilian Blowout
Professional keratin smoothing treatment that eliminates frizz and reduces curl for up to 12 weeks. Cymonne customizes the formula to your hair's current condition — not a one-size approach pulled off a shelf. Results are a gradual wash-out, so hair returns to its natural state without a harsh line of demarcation. Works on natural hair and on hair with extensions already in place. Cymonne always considers current hair health before recommending.

Custom Color
Color built around your skin undertone, lifestyle, and the current health of your strands. Whether dimensional highlights, rich single-process depth, or a precise shade to blend seamlessly with extension hair — Cymonne approaches color as craft, not formula.
