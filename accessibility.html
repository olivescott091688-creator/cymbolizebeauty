§ Accessibility Statement

Beauty should be
accessible to everyone.

CymbolizeBeauty is committed to making this website usable by all people — regardless of ability, technology, or circumstance.

§ 01 Our Commitment
01/03
Our Commitment

Everyone deserves
a seat in the chair.

CymbolizeBeauty serves women navigating hair loss — many of whom are also navigating medical treatment, chronic conditions, or disability. Accessibility isn't an afterthought for us; it's part of who we serve. We strive to ensure this website conforms to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and we continue to improve the experience for every visitor.

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. As this website evolves, we review new pages and features to keep them usable for everyone.

§ 02 Website Accessibility Features
02/03
Built In

Accessibility
features.

  • ◆  Semantic HTML structure with proper headings and landmarks for screen readers
  • ◆  Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • ◆  Keyboard-navigable menus, accordions, and forms
  • ◆  Reduced-motion support — animations are disabled automatically when your device requests it
  • ◆  Readable font sizes and a layout that adapts to phones, tablets, and desktops
  • ◆  Color contrast reviewed across light and dark sections
  • ◆  Videos play without sound and never rely on audio to convey information
  • ◆  Descriptive link text and clear, consistent navigation on every page
§ 03 Need Assistance
03/03
We're Here

Need help or found
a barrier?

If you experience any difficulty using this website, or if any part of it is not accessible to you, please reach out. We take every report seriously and will work to resolve the issue — and we're happy to provide any information from this site in an alternative format.

Email Support@cymbolizebeauty.com or message @cymbolizebeauty on Instagram, and Cymonne will personally make sure you get what you need.
