By Service

What you'll
find here.

Six service categories — each with before and afters currently being photographed and prepared for this gallery.

01
Mesh Integration
Full before-and-after transformations on hair loss clients. Alopecia, GLP-1 thinning, chemo recovery, and diffuse loss — every pattern of loss looks different, and the results are equally distinct. Photos coming soon.
02
Invisible Bead Wefts
Volume and length transformations for fine-hair clients. You'll see the result in ponytails, updos, and down — because the real test of an IBW install is whether it holds up in every style. Photos coming soon.
03
K-Tips
Individual strand placements that add density without bulk. Side-by-side comparisons showing how individual strand placement differs from weft methods — and why it creates the most natural blending. Photos coming soon.
04
Custom Color
Color transformations built around skin undertone and hair condition — dimensional highlights, single-process depth, and extension blending. Before and afters that show color as enhancement rather than change. Photos coming soon.
05
Brazilian Blowout
Frizz-to-smooth transformations — same day before-and-afters that demonstrate exactly how keratin smoothing changes the texture and manageability of different hair types. Photos coming soon.
