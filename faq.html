About the Technique

Mesh Integration:
the basics.

Mesh Integration is a hair extension technique specifically designed for women experiencing hair loss, thinning, or alopecia. A fine, breathable mesh base is placed against the scalp and custom human hair is woven or attached through it — creating a natural-looking system that adds fullness, length, and coverage regardless of how sparse your existing hair is. Unlike traditional extensions, it doesn't require a dense base of hair to work from. It works on virtually any hair density, including complete hair loss.
No — they're fundamentally different. A wig sits on top of your head and can be removed. Mesh Integration is attached directly to your scalp and becomes part of how you wear your hair every day. It moves with you, washes like your own hair, and holds up in every style — ponytail, updo, or down. Clients consistently describe it as indistinguishable from their natural hair.
With proper care and regular maintenance appointments, Mesh Integration can last for many months. The human hair used is durable and can last a year or more with good care. Maintenance visits are needed periodically — typically every 6–10 weeks — to adjust the positioning as your natural hair grows and the mesh shifts. Cymonne will walk you through exactly what to expect during your consultation.

The system is built to live with you, not just sit on top of you. Cymonne will give you detailed care instructions so you know exactly how to wash, dry, and maintain it between appointments.
When installed correctly by a trained specialist, Mesh Integration should not damage your hair. Cymonne's focus is always on the health of your natural hair first. Clients regularly report that their hair is in better condition after working with Cymonne — because Cymonne takes the time to assess, treat, and plan for your hair's long-term health. Practical Details

Cost, maintenance
& logistics.

Pricing is discussed at consultation because every system is built from scratch to each client's specific hair loss pattern, density, and goals. There is no standard price list — the cost depends on the extent of coverage needed, the amount of hair used, and the complexity of the build. Cymonne gives you a clear, honest price quote during your consultation with no surprise add-ons. She'll also walk you through the cost of maintenance visits so you can plan accordingly.
Mesh Integration maintenance is typically every 6–10 weeks, depending on your hair's growth rate. Invisible Bead Wefts are generally every 6–8 weeks. K-Tips go longer — typically every 3–4 months. During maintenance, Cymonne adjusts the positioning of the system as your natural hair grows, checks the integrity of the installation, and makes any repairs needed. Regular maintenance is essential to both the longevity of your system and the health of your natural hair underneath.
Traditional extensions installed incorrectly on fragile hair can absolutely cause traction alopecia or increased shedding — and that's exactly why choosing a trained specialist matters. Cymonne uses methods specifically designed to add coverage without stressing your existing hair. Mesh Integration and Invisible Bead Wefts are both gentle, low-traction approaches. If your hair's current condition means any extension work would cause harm, Cymonne will tell you that honestly rather than proceed.
The human hair used in Mesh Integration and IBW systems can be heat-styled and, in many cases, colored — but Cymonne will advise you specifically on what's safe for your system. She often handles color herself to ensure the extension hair blends perfectly with your natural color. She'll give you clear guidelines during your consultation and at install so you know exactly what you can and can't do between appointments.
