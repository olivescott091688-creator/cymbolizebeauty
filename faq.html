About Mesh Integration

The Technique

Mesh Integration is a hair extension technique specifically designed for women experiencing hair loss, thinning, or alopecia. A fine, breathable mesh base is placed against the scalp and custom human hair is woven or attached through it — creating a natural-looking system that adds fullness, length, and coverage regardless of how sparse your existing hair is. Unlike traditional extensions, it doesn't require a dense base of hair to work from.

Yes — it was designed with hair loss clients in mind. Mesh Integration does not put traction or stress on fragile existing hair the way traditional extension methods can. The mesh base distributes weight evenly and doesn't require pulling or braiding your natural strands tightly. It's one of the gentlest options available for clients with significant hair loss or alopecia. Cymonne always begins with a thorough assessment to confirm you're a good candidate.

When installed correctly by a trained specialist, Mesh Integration should not damage your hair. Cymonne's focus is always on the health of your natural hair first. Clients regularly report that their hair is in better condition after working with Cymonne — not because of the extensions themselves, but because Cymonne takes the time to assess, treat, and plan for your hair's long-term health. She'll only proceed if she's confident the installation is appropriate for your current hair condition.

The initial consultation and installation can take several hours depending on the extent of hair loss and complexity of your system. Cymonne is known for thoroughness — she takes the time to get the assessment right, customize the hair to match yours exactly, and ensure the installation is flawless. She can sometimes complete a full build in a single same-day appointment. She'll give you a realistic time estimate during your consultation.

With proper care and regular maintenance appointments, Mesh Integration can last for months. The hair itself is durable human hair that can last a year or more with good care. Maintenance visits are needed periodically to adjust the positioning as your natural hair grows. Cymonne will walk you through exactly what to expect during your consultation.

Hair Loss Clients

Specific Conditions

Absolutely. Alopecia — whether areata, androgenetic, or traction-related — is one of Cymonne's primary specialties. Mesh Integration was originally developed with alopecia in mind because it doesn't rely on continuous hair coverage to work. The mesh base fills in patchy or sparse areas and blends seamlessly. Every alopecia case is unique, so a consultation is the best first step to understand what's possible for your specific pattern.

GLP-1 hair loss (from Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, and similar medications) is something Cymonne sees increasingly often. This type of hair loss is typically diffuse — affecting overall density rather than specific patches — and is often temporary once medication is stabilized. Mesh Integration is frequently a great fit because it addresses overall thinning across the scalp. Cymonne will assess your current hair density and recommend the best approach for your situation.

Yes, with care and the right timing. Cymonne approaches chemo hair loss clients with particular sensitivity and flexibility. The best solution depends on where you are in treatment, your scalp's current sensitivity, and how much hair remains. Reach out for a consultation — Cymonne will be honest with you about what's possible and what the best timeline looks like. You don't have to navigate this alone.
