What you need
to know.

Mesh Integration is a hair extension technique specifically designed for women experiencing hair loss, thinning, or alopecia. A fine, breathable mesh base is placed against the scalp and custom human hair is woven or attached through it — creating a natural-looking system that adds fullness, length, and coverage regardless of how sparse your existing hair is. Unlike traditional extensions, it doesn't require a dense base of hair to work from.
They're related, but there's a key difference: a wig typically isn't customized to your head. A Mesh Integration system is completely customizable — built to your scalp, your measurements, and matched to your color and texture. And depending on how your system is designed, it can even be removable: some systems stay secured between appointments, while others are built as "free wear" systems that can be taken off and put back on. Cymonne will help you decide which design fits your lifestyle at your consultation.
Yes — you can live your life in your system, including working out. But it's important to have honest expectations: the hair in your system is not your real hair, so it can't be washed or treated the same way. It's delicate, and it needs its own care routine to stay beautiful and last. Some system designs also aren't suited for certain styles, like high ponytails — it depends on how yours is built. Cymonne gives every client detailed care instructions and will be upfront about exactly what your specific system can and can't do.
When installed correctly by a trained specialist, Mesh Integration should not damage your hair. Cymonne's focus is always on the health of your natural hair first. She'll only proceed if she's confident the installation is appropriate for your current hair condition. If it's not, she'll tell you.
Pricing is discussed at consultation because every system is built from scratch to each client's specific hair loss pattern, density, and goals. Cymonne gives you a clear, honest quote with no surprise add-ons — and will walk you through the cost of maintenance visits so you can plan accordingly.
