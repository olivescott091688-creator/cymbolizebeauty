Who I Help

You're not
alone in this.

Four distinct patterns of hair loss. Four completely tailored approaches. Every consultation begins with an honest assessment — not a template.

01
GLP-1 Medication
Hair Loss
Ozempic, Wegovy, and Mounjaro cause diffuse thinning that spreads across the full scalp. Cymonne specializes in coverage solutions built specifically for this pattern — adding density across the whole head. This type of loss is also often temporary, which factors into treatment planning.
02
Chemotherapy &
Treatment
Hair loss during cancer treatment is one of the most emotionally significant experiences a woman can go through. Cymonne approaches every chemo client with particular sensitivity and flexible timing, using methods specifically designed for sensitive or reactive scalps.
03
Alopecia
Areata, androgenetic, or traction alopecia — each presents differently. Mesh Integration was designed with alopecia clients in mind because it doesn't require a continuous base of hair. It works directly from the scalp, regardless of how patchy or sparse your existing hair may be.
04
Hormonal &
Fine Hair
Postpartum shedding, perimenopause, thyroid-related thinning, or naturally fine and fragile hair — traditional extensions often cause more damage than help. Cymonne works exclusively with methods that add volume without putting mechanical stress on vulnerable strands.

✦ Signature Specialty

Mesh
Integration.

A fine, breathable mesh base — custom-matched to your scalp tone — is placed gently against the scalp. Human hair is woven individually through the mesh openings, building coverage directly from the scalp outward.

It works on virtually any density — including areas of visible scalp that traditional extensions cannot reach. No traction on fragile strands. No heat or adhesive at the attachment point.

The result moves, washes, and behaves exactly like natural hair. Clients consistently describe it as impossible to detect.
