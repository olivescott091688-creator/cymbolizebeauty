The Founder

Meet Cymonne.

Cymonne is the founder of CymbolizeBeauty and an Elevate-certified Mesh Integration specialist in Las Vegas. Her studio was built for the women most stylists don't know how to help — women experiencing hair loss from GLP-1 medications, chemotherapy, hormones, alopecia, and trichotillomania, and women with naturally fine, fragile hair.

What she's most proud of isn't the ranking or the reviews. It's helping the women other stylists have turned away because of their hair loss — with a mission that hasn't changed since day one: to hand a woman back the version of herself that hair loss tried to erase.

Her Story

It started with her grandmother.

"I didn't even know she wore a wig — she never took it off in front of anyone."

One day, a young Cymonne found her grandmother crying. When she finally told the truth, it was this: she felt ugly. She didn't feel like a woman without the wig — and even though it was uncomfortable and painful to wear, she was too ashamed to take it off.

That was the moment that set the course of Cymonne's life. She decided right then she would become a hairstylist who gave women back their confidence and self-esteem.
