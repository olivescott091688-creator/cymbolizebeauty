Before You Book

Cancellation
policy.

Our goal is to provide quality care in a timely manner. Appointments are in high demand, and an early cancellation gives another woman the opportunity to access timely care.

01

At the time of booking, you'll be asked to pay a deposit that is credited toward your treatment. Time has been specifically reserved for your appointment.

02

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please do so at least 24 hours before your appointment — your deposit will be transferred to a future appointment.

03

Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice forfeit the deposit.
