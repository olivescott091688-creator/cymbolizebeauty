Cymonne's Specialties

Five types of hair loss.
Five tailored approaches.

Every type of hair loss presents differently — in pattern, in texture change, in emotional weight. Each one requires a different plan.

01
GLP-1 Medication
Hair Loss
Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, and other GLP-1 agonists cause diffuse thinning that spreads across the full scalp rather than appearing in patches. Cymonne specializes in coverage solutions built specifically for this pattern — adding density across the whole head, not just targeted spots. This type of loss is also often temporary once medication is stabilized, which factors into treatment planning. Early intervention is always better than waiting.
02
Chemotherapy &
Treatment Hair Loss
Hair loss during cancer treatment is one of the most emotionally significant experiences a woman can go through. Cymonne approaches every chemo client with particular sensitivity, flexible timing based on where you are in treatment or recovery, and methods specifically designed for sensitive or reactive scalps. Timing matters — she'll help you understand the best approach for your specific stage, and she'll meet you exactly where you are.
03
Alopecia
Areata, androgenetic, traction — each presents differently and requires a completely tailored approach. Mesh Integration was designed with alopecia clients in mind precisely because it doesn't require a continuous base of hair to attach to. It works directly from the scalp, regardless of how patchy or sparse your existing hair may be. Every alopecia case is unique, and Cymonne has worked with virtually every pattern and stage.
04
Hormonal &
Fine Hair Thinning
Postpartum shedding, perimenopause, thyroid-related thinning, stress-induced shedding, or naturally fine and fragile hair — traditional extensions often cause more damage than help for these clients. Cymonne works exclusively with methods that add volume and length without putting mechanical stress on vulnerable strands. The goal is always to support hair health, not compromise it.
05
Trichotillomania
A hair-pulling condition that often leaves localized patches of loss — and carries a weight that goes far beyond the physical. Cymonne's studio is a completely judgment-free space. Integration systems are especially well suited here: they provide seamless coverage over affected areas, create a gentle physical barrier that protects regrowth, and give you back a full head of hair while your natural hair recovers underneath. Every plan is built around your pattern, your pace, and your comfort.

The Problem

Why traditional extensions
don't work for hair loss.

Most extension methods were designed for clients who already have a healthy, full head of hair. Hair loss clients don't fit that assumption — and that's where things go wrong.

Traditional extensions — keratin bonds, tape-ins, clip-ins, standard sew-ins — require:

✕
A dense base of hair to anchor into or sew against
✕
Hair strong enough to withstand the weight of wefts or bonds
✕
Enough coverage to hide attachment points from underneath
✕
Strands resilient enough to be sectioned, pulled, and braided tightly

For women experiencing hair loss, none of those things may be true. A well-meaning stylist who doesn't know how to adapt can actually cause more damage than help.

What hair loss clients are told:

→ "I can't work with that."

→ Extensions that fall out or look unnatural

→ Methods that cause more traction hair loss

→ "Wait until it grows back" — with no guidance

This is what Cymonne built her practice to solve.

The Solution

The Mesh Integration
difference.

A system specifically designed for the client that traditional methods can't help.

Mesh Integration uses a fine, breathable mesh base that sits gently against the scalp. Human hair — individual strands or weft panels — is woven through the mesh, creating a natural-looking system that:

✦
Works on virtually any hair density — even very sparse or completely bald areas
✦
Does not require a dense base to attach to — anchors to the scalp directly
✦
Is completely breathable — scalp health is maintained, not compromised
✦
Is completely customizable — built to your head, your measurements, your color and texture
✦
Can be designed as a "free wear" system that's removable — or secured between appointments
✦
Does not put traction or stress on existing fragile strands
✦
Custom-matched to your exact scalp tone, hair color, and texture

Cymonne doesn't oversell results. She gives every client the truth — which is the only foundation for real confidence.

01

The Consultation

Every journey starts with a thorough scalp and density assessment. Cymonne evaluates your pattern, your existing hair condition, and your relevant history. She explains every option clearly and recommends a plan based on what she actually finds — not what's easiest or most profitable. No pressure. No obligation.

02

The Build

Initial installations can take several hours depending on complexity. Cymonne is known for thoroughness — she takes the time to get it right. Most clients can complete a full build in a single same-day appointment. She'll give you a realistic time estimate at consultation, not a number designed to sell you on booking.

03

When to Seek Help

If your hair loss is sudden, severe, or accompanied by other symptoms — see a dermatologist or physician first. Cymonne is a hair specialist, not a medical provider. She'll always tell you honestly if she thinks medical evaluation should come before any cosmetic treatment. Your health first, always.
